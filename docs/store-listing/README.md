## Full description

```
NepalGo connects you with a small team in Kathmandu who plan every trip by hand – real people who know the trails, the lodges and the weather.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Explore destinations with photos, a day-by-day itinerary, current weather with packing tips, and permit and route notes.
2. Request a trip: choose your arrival date and group size, and add a guide or porter if you like. Tell us anything special in your own words.
3. We check availability with local partners and send you an itemised bill in US dollars – every hotel night, transfer and guide day on its own line. No surprises.
4. Review your final itinerary and the key policies (cancellation tiers, weather and force majeure, identity), then pay through a secure Payoneer link and upload your payment confirmation in the app.
5. Once we verify your payment, your trip is confirmed. Show the voucher QR code to your hotel, driver or guide.

WHAT YOU GET
• Itineraries planned by a local team, not an algorithm
• Clear, itemised bills in USD and a tax invoice by e-mail after payment
• A "pay by" date on every bill, so you always know your deadline
• Notifications when your bill is ready and when your trip is confirmed
• NepalGo Travel Credit in your wallet when a cancellation entitles you to it
• Help on WhatsApp from the same team that plans your trip
• Reviews from travellers who actually completed the trip
• Sign in with e-mail or Google; light and dark mode

YOUR DATA
We collect only what a booking needs. No ads, and we never sell your data. Card details never reach the app – payment happens on Payoneer. You can delete your account at any time in Profile; tax records of paid trips are kept as the law requires.

Booking requires travellers aged 18 or over. Trekking permits are arranged separately; the permit notes in the app are general guidance.
```

## Screenshots

Play needs 2–8 phone screenshots (portrait, 9:16 works; min 320 px, max 3840 px on a side). Suggested set, in this order:

1. Explore feed with destination photos
2. Destination page: photo slideshow and itinerary
3. Trip request form (dates, group size, guide/porter)
4. Itemised bill
5. Trip "Ready to pay" with the pay-by banner
6. Confirmed trip with voucher QR
7. Inbox / notification

Before capturing: replace the Everest cover photo with one you own or have a licence for, use a demo customer (generic name), and use realistic content. Then, with the emulator showing each screen:

```powershell
cd mobile-app
.\tool\capture-screenshot.ps1 01-explore      # saves docs\store-listing\screenshots\01-explore.png
```
